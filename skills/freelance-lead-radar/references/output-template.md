## Proposal rules

1. **Specific opener** — quote a detail from the JD, not a generic compliment.
2. **Real proof** — only the user's actual experience, no invented metrics.
3. **Small ask** — a question or a proposed first step, not "hire me".
4. **Under 120 words** — clients skim.
5. **No attachments unless asked** — link to portfolio only if relevant.
6. **Language** — match the job post language.

## Bad proposal patterns (never use)

- "I am the best fit for this job."
- "I have 10 years of experience" without proof.
- "I can do this perfectly."
- Copy-pasting the same proposal to every job.
- Inventing clients, metrics, or testimonials.
- Offering free work to "prove" skill.
